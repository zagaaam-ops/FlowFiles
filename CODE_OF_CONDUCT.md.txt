# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.

We welcome contributions regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Be constructive.
- Be patient.
- Focus on solving problems.
- Welcome newcomers.
- Share knowledge openly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination.
- Trolling or inflammatory comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any behavior that makes others feel unsafe.

## Project Values

FlowFiles values:

- Collaboration
- Professionalism
- Transparency
- Quality
- Respect

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from participating in the project.

## Contact

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainer through GitHub.
